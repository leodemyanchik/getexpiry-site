Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 16, 2026

At Expiry, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide and improve our smart coupon and expiration management services:

  • Account Information: When you sign in (via Email, Google Sign-In, or Sign in with Apple), we collect your email address and authentication identifier to create and sync your account.
  • Coupon & Receipt Data: Information you input manually or scan using OCR, such as merchant names, coupon descriptions, discount values, expiration dates, barcodes, and images.
  • Camera & Photo Access: With your permission, we access your device camera or photo library to scan barcodes and process receipt images for text recognition (OCR).
  • Local Storage: Your coupon data is cached locally on your device using encrypted/secure local storage for offline access.
  • Notification Tokens: Device notification identifiers used solely to send you local and push notifications for coupon expiration alerts.
  • App Usage Analytics: When you are signed in, the app records a limited set of usage events together with your account identifier and a random app-installation identifier. See In-app analytics below.
  • Ad Attribution (iPhone): Whether the app was installed from an Apple Ads campaign, as verified with Apple. See Apple Ads attribution below.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly for the following purposes:

  • To provide, manage, and personalize your coupon and expiration reminders.
  • To process receipt images using Optical Character Recognition (OCR).
  • To synchronize your data across your signed-in devices via cloud storage.
  • To send timely expiration reminders and notifications.
  • To provide customer support and respond to your requests.
  • To understand which features are used, measure whether people return to the app, and improve Expiry (analytics).

3. Data Processing & Third-Party Services

We work with trusted third-party service providers to power key features of Expiry:

  • Supabase: Used for secure user authentication, database management, and cloud synchronization.
  • OpenAI API: When you request AI import, the receipt or coupon image is sent through our server to OpenAI to extract the fields shown for your review. When you request an AI card cover, we send only your selected style, palette and category, not your saved card image, codes or account details. Our server does not log these images, prompts or provider responses. AI import requests use the API's non-stored response option; provider-side processing and retention remain subject to OpenAI's API data controls.
  • Apple / Google OAuth: Used solely to verify your identity during sign-in.
  • Store logos (Hunter, Google): To show a store's logo, the app may turn the store name into a web address (for example, "amazon.com") and request that site's public logo from Hunter's logo service or Google's favicon service. Only this address is sent, never your codes, amounts, dates, notes or account details.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties or advertising networks.

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Your data is retained for as long as your account remains active. You have full control over your data:

  • Delete Coupons: You can edit or delete individual coupons at any time inside the app.
  • Account Deletion: You can delete your account and all associated cloud data directly from the app (Settings → Account → Delete Account) or by contacting our support team. Account deletion permanently purges your data from our servers.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures, including HTTPS encryption in transit and Row-Level Security (RLS) policies in cloud storage, to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.

6. Children's Privacy

Expiry is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

In-app analytics

The Expiry mobile app collects first-party product analytics to understand which features are used and whether people come back to the app. When you are signed in, the app records these events: opening the app, opening a saved card or coupon code, and tapping or opening an expiry reminder. Our servers also record when coupons, cards and reminders are created, marked as used or unused, or deleted.

Each record contains your account identifier, a random identifier created for this installation of the app, a session identifier, the platform (iOS or Android), timestamps and the type of event. Records that relate to a specific card, coupon or reminder store only its internal identifier, not its title, merchant, code, barcode, images or notes. This analytics data is linked to your account.

Analytics data is stored with our database provider Supabase. We do not sell it, share it with advertising networks or data brokers, or use it to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. The app does not access your device's advertising identifier. If you are offline, events are kept on your device for up to 30 days and sent when a connection is available. Analytics records are kept while your account is active and are deleted together with your account when you delete it.

Apple Ads attribution

On iPhone, Expiry uses Apple's AdServices framework to learn whether the app was installed after someone tapped or viewed an Expiry ad in the App Store, so we can measure which ad campaigns bring people who actually use the app. The app asks iOS for an attribution token and sends it to our server, which checks it with Apple. Apple returns whether the install is attributed to an Apple Ads campaign and, if so, identifiers for the campaign, ad group, keyword and ad, the country or region, the type of conversion and the date of the ad interaction.

We store this result and, once you sign in, link it to your account. The token itself is not stored. This does not use your device's advertising identifier, does not ask for permission to track you, and is not combined with data from other companies' apps or websites. Apple Ads does not receive your account details or wallet content from us. Attribution records are deleted together with your account; results that are never linked to an account are deleted after about 35 days.

Website analytics and cookies

This section applies to the public website. For analytics inside the mobile app, see In-app analytics. With your consent, we use Google Analytics 4, provided by Google, to measure visits, traffic sources, pages viewed and clicks leading to our App Store listing. These clicks do not establish that an app was installed.

Google Analytics uses first-party cookies to distinguish browsers and sessions. Data can include a pseudonymous browser identifier, browser/device information, approximate location derived by Google from the connection, public page paths and campaign labels. Google receives network connection information when analytics loads. We do not send coupon content, account details, private coupon-link tokens, arbitrary URL query strings or fragments. Our coupon-sharing and sign-in pages do not load this counter. Advertising personalization and Google Signals are not enabled by our website integration.

Analytics is off until you choose “Allow analytics”. Choosing “No analytics” does not prevent access to the website. You can reopen “Analytics settings” in the footer at any time to withdraw consent; this stops further collection by this counter and clears its first-party analytics cookies. Withdrawal does not erase information already collected. We remember your consent choice in browser local storage for up to 180 days; analytics cookies are configured to expire after 90 days without extending their expiration on each visit. Blocking browser storage may prevent your choice from being remembered.

Data is processed by Google and may be processed outside your country. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services and Google’s Privacy Policy. Contact support@getexpiry.me with questions or requests about website data.
