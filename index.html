Ask us something else ↗
Does Expiry find new discounts?

No — it helps you organize coupons and vouchers you already have. Think paper coupons, discounts from your inbox, and saved screenshots.

Is it only for bottle-return vouchers?

Not at all. Deposit vouchers are one useful example. You can also save shopping coupons, discount vouchers and plain promo codes.

Can I throw away the paper coupon?

Keep the original until you know the retailer accepts a digital code. Expiry stores your coupon, but each shop decides how it can be redeemed. Always check its terms.

Do I need to check the scanned details?

Yes. Recognition can make mistakes. Review the amount, code and expiry date before saving, and check any minimum spend or other conditions on the original coupon.

Which devices and languages are supported?

Expiry is available on the App Store for iPhone. The app includes English, Polish and Russian. There is no Android version at the moment.
